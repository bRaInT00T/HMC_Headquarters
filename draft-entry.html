Draft Day Entry

The three things this page is for while the draft runs: keep Yahoo syncing, record picks, and run the clock. Pre-draft setup — draft order, keepers, trades, colors — is on Settings.

Settings

1. Yahoo Sync

If the league is drafting inside Yahoo, leave live sync on and this tab open — picks land on the board on their own, and the form below is only needed to fix one. Connecting the Yahoo account is a one-time step on Settings.

2. Record a Pick

Fallback for leagues not drafting in Yahoo, or to fix a synced pick. Player Name matches loosely — typos, apostrophes and initials are all forgiven, and last name first is fine ("jamar chase", "ajbrown" and "chase jamarr" all land on Ja'Marr Chase). Position and NFL Team narrow that list, and fill in on their own once you pick a player from it. Position lists our roster slots, not raw NFL positions — W/R/T spans WR, RB and TE, and DEF offers the 32 team defenses by name (individual defenders aren't draftable). The form opens on whichever pick is on the clock and advances in snake order after each save (stepping back on Undo). To correct an earlier pick, set Pick # to it — Round, Team and the recorded player load in, and saving overwrites that pick rather than appending a new one.
